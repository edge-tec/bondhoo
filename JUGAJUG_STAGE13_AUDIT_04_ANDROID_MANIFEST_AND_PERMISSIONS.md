## 3. Exported Component Security & Intent Filter Audit

To prevent unauthorized inter-process communication (IPC) attacks:
1. **MainActivity:**  
   `android:name="com.connectly.android.MainActivity"`  
   `android:exported="true"` (Required for launcher).  
   Intent filters: `android.intent.action.MAIN` (Launcher) and deep link scheme `connectly://`.
2. **Push Service:**  
   `com.connectly.android.feature.notifications.data.fcm.ConnectlyFirebaseMessagingService`  
   `android:exported="false"` (Protected from external intent spoofing).
3. **Call Broadcast Receiver:**  
   `com.connectly.android.core.webrtc.notification.CallActionBroadcastReceiver`  
   `android:exported="false"` (Actions `ACTION_ACCEPT_CALL`, `ACTION_REJECT_CALL`, `ACTION_END_CALL` restricted to local application broadcasts).
4. **App Provider:**  
   `androidx.startup.InitializationProvider`  
   `android:exported="false"`.
