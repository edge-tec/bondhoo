## 3. Bundle Processing & Warning Review

When `app-release.aab` is processed by Google Play Console:
1. **Split APK Delivery:**
   - Play Feature Delivery generates optimized APKs (language-specific resources, ABI binaries, screen density drawables).
   - Download size for end users drops from 47 MB (universal APK) to approximately **18–25 MB** depending on device architecture.
2. **Permission Review:**
   - Clean permissions list (Internet, Camera, Audio, Media, Biometric, Notifications).
   - Zero restricted permission warnings (no Accessibility, no Device Admin, no Background Location).
3. **Android 16 Readiness:**
   - Target SDK 36 is recognized without platform deprecation blocks.

## 4. Tester Onboarding & Opt-In Protocol

### Steps for Testers:
1. The project owner creates an email list (e.g., `Bondhoo Internal QA`) containing tester Gmail/Google Workspace accounts.
2. The owner shares the **Join on Android** or **Join on the Web** testing URL.
3. The tester clicks the link and clicks **"Accept invite / Become a tester"**.
4. The tester opens the Google Play Store on their device and clicks **"Install"** to download the official Play-distributed build.

*Note: Sideloading the standalone APK is maintained for preliminary lab validation, but does not replace the requirement for verifying Play App Signing on real Play-distributed builds.*
